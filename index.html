Rules & Punishments Guide

What happens if a rule gets broken, explained simply. Everyone starts with a clean slate, and most rules give you a chance to fix things before anything serious happens.

How to read this

Three things to know before you look at the chart.

Offenses go in stepsEach rule has up to four steps. Step 1 is the first time you break it, step 2 the second time, and so on. Repeating the same mistake means a stronger punishment.
Minor vs MajorMinor rules are everyday slip-ups and start with a warning. Major rules are serious, so they skip the warnings and start with a mute, kick, or ban.
“N/A” means doneIf a step says N/A, there is no next step because the previous one is already the final punishment, usually a permanent ban.

Good to know

Example. You post the same message five times in a row. That is Spam. The first time you get a verbal warning. If it happens again you get an official warning and a 1 hour mute. A third time is a kick, and a fourth time is a 3 day temporary ban.
Not sure about something? Ask a staff member. We would much rather answer a question than hand out a punishment.
Think a punishment was unfair? Message a staff member calmly and we will take another look. Arguing in public channels counts as disrupting conversations.
